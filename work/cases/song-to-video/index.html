This is an evidence checklist and a route to a project brief. It is not a demo or a claimed client outcome.

The status is pending

A finished song-to-analysis-to-video case must connect a real, approved source song to the analysis decisions and a playable final video. That chain has not been established as a cleared public example.

JonaAudio and the pinned RhythmCut preview provide pieces of the workflow. Their existence, an empty editor, a synthetic audio fixture or an invented mockup is not proof of a completed commercial video.

What a publishable example needs

  1. Cleared inputs. A song, footage and artwork with permission to show the relevant material and credit its contributors.
  2. Traceable analysis. The source, estimated tempo/key or timing observations, any artist corrections and the decisions that informed the edit.
  3. A real export. A playable final video inspected for sync, content, aspect ratio and agreed delivery requirements.
  4. Accurate attribution. Song, artist, production, camera, editing and other collaborators credited according to the actual work.
  5. An honest outcome. The observed result and its limits, with no invented commercial metrics or substitutions for missing evidence.
