## S3 — Music world
- Opt-in official Spotify artist embed; existing Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube exits. No third-party iframe loaded until explicitly requested.
- Local-device audio selection supports play/pause, seek, volume, mute, queue and track switching. Temporary Blob URLs revoked on exit. Neither audio bytes nor filenames are uploaded, and local filenames are not persisted.
- Rights-gated catalog/renderers for approved public tracks, releases and videos. Every preview requires a public media URL under the designated media path plus explicit sitePreviewApproved. Release and video cards require distinct approval.
- Public-track resume is paused and same-browser only; playback does not continue between full-page navigation. Unknown codec playback falls back to official stream when provided.
- BLOCKED: no specific cleared/public audio tracks, artwork credits, release details or video permalinks supplied. No public-song playback or commercial-media device acceptance is claimed.
- BLOCKED: public email follow/subscribe and unsubscribe provider not selected or tested. Official platform follows only.

## S4 — Return and freshness
- Optional local history starts disabled. Only stable public registry IDs are stored, capped at 12 recents and 32 favorites; no filenames, contacts or source-app media/project IDs.
- My Universe /continue/ displays favorite/recents, scoped app recovery wording, removal and Clear All. Clear All removes only site memory and public-track resume, not app-owned IndexedDB/OPFS.
- JonaAudio explicitly needs relinking to original audio; RhythmCut projects are owned and resumed inside its app. BeatCut remains a project-status path.
- Curated /updates/ and home preview display only reviewed public releases (S1/S2 and JonaAudio M6), backed by linked PRs/commits; no raw commit feed or unverified S3/S4 deploy claim.

## How to add cleared recordings
1. Publish only owner-cleared final preview audio derivatives under assets/music/ (never stems, unreleased drafts or private client media).
2. Add stable track IDs, title, artist, optional release and public streaming link to assets/music-catalog.json. Preview entries also need previewUrl and rights.sitePreviewApproved true. Keep permission/credit source outside the public manifest as appropriate.
3. Release entries require rights.publicMetadataApproved true; video entries rights.publicVideoApproved true. Validate URLs and site/app media performance before marking them published.
4. Newsletter and show-subscription delivery requires a genuine opt-in/unsubscribe provider before activation.
