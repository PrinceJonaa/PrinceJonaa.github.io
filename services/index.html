01 / Audio

Music mixing
& mastering.

Shape an artist-approved mix or master around the song, the source material and the release requirements.

Request a written quote →

Deliverables

  • The mix or master formats named in the quote.
  • Instrumental, clean, performance or alternate versions only if included.
  • A review process and revision scope agreed before work begins.

What I need from you

  • A brief, reference tracks and release requirements.
  • Organized stems for a mix, or a stereo mix for mastering.
  • Confirmation that you can authorize use of the source audio.

Scope & exclusions

  • Recording, beat production, editing or damaged-stem repair unless included.
  • Distribution, playlist placement and commercial-result guarantees.

Revisions: The quote sets the number of revision rounds and what each round covers. New stems, a new song or a change in direction may require a revised quote.

Price & schedule: Confirmed in the written quote after reviewing the brief and source requirements.

02 / Visuals

Music video
& creative editing.

Turn a clear creative direction and cleared media into a release visual with an agreed runtime and delivery target.

Request a written quote →

Deliverables

  • An edited video at the runtime, aspect ratios and formats in the quote.
  • Titles, captions, artwork animation or social cutdowns only if included.
  • Review exports and agreed final delivery materials.

What I need from you

  • An approved song, concept and visual references.
  • Cleared footage, artwork and requested credits.
  • Target platforms, export requirements and an agreed access plan.

Scope & exclusions

  • Filming, travel, locations, cast or licensed assets unless included.
  • An automatic song-to-video result, unapproved footage use or guaranteed audience growth.

Revisions: The quote defines review rounds and changes within the agreed concept. Reshoots, new footage or a new creative direction are separately scoped.

Price & schedule: Confirmed in the written quote after reviewing the brief and source requirements.

03 / Engineering

Websites
& software.

Build a public interface or focused workflow around the actions your audience actually needs to take.

Request a written quote →

Deliverables

  • The pages, workflows, features and integrations listed in the quote.
  • A responsive interface and agreed acceptance checks.
  • Source, deployment, documentation or handoff materials as specified.

What I need from you

  • The problem, audience, goal and required user actions.
  • Approved copy, branding, media and content ownership.
  • Integration requirements and authorized access when needed.

Scope & exclusions

  • Hosting, domains, subscriptions, transaction fees and ongoing maintenance unless included.
  • Unscoped integrations, legal copy, guaranteed sales or unapproved access to systems.

Revisions: Review rounds and acceptance criteria are set in the quote. Extra features, new integrations and ongoing support require an explicit additional scope.

Price & schedule: Confirmed in the written quote after reviewing the brief and source requirements.
